BEHIND THE ATLAS

Methodology

  1. Collected 2025–26 NBA season data: traditional box-score, passing, and catch-and-shoot stats.
  2. Excluded players with fewer than 500 minutes, leaving 389 players.
  3. Cleaned and combined player records, then adjusted stats so players with different minutes and shot volumes could be compared.
  4. Split players at 25% usage so offensive engines and specialists were clustered separately.
  5. Weighted features by how much they matter when describing a player’s role in real-life NBA discourse.
  6. For role players, evaluated 5–12 K-means clusters, ruled out groups below 4% of the pool, and chose the eligible result with the best silhouette score.
  7. Examined each cluster’s members and shared traits to name its archetype.
